# VidKit

### A simple, standalone video toolkit powered by FFmpeg.

VidKit is a lightweight desktop application built to make common video-processing tasks easier without requiring a full video editor or complicated command-line workflows.

It runs locally on your computer and uses the power of **FFmpeg** underneath, giving you reliable video processing while keeping the experience simple and accessible.

---

## ✦ Why VidKit?

Sometimes you don't need a full video editor.

You just need to perform one specific video task quickly, without opening a massive application, uploading your files to a website, or figuring out an FFmpeg command.

That's where VidKit comes in.

- 🎬 **Simple video tooling**
- ⚡ **Powered by FFmpeg**
- 🖥️ **Standalone Windows application**
- 🔒 **Local processing**
- ☁️ **No cloud upload required**
- 🛠️ **Designed to grow into a collection of useful video utilities**

## ⚙️ Powered by FFmpeg

VidKit uses **FFmpeg**, one of the most widely used multimedia frameworks for video and audio processing.

FFmpeg provides the underlying processing capabilities while VidKit focuses on providing a simpler desktop experience around them.

## 📦 Installation

VidKit is distributed as a standalone executable.

1. Download `VidKit.v1.0.exe` from the latest release.
2. Place it wherever you want.
3. Launch the executable.
4. Start using VidKit.

No complicated installation process is required.

---

## 🔐 Privacy

VidKit is designed around local processing.

Your video files do not need to be uploaded to a cloud service for processing.

Your files stay on your computer unless you choose to move or share them yourself.

---

## 🛣️ Roadmap

VidKit is still in its early stages.

Future releases may expand the toolkit with additional video utilities, improved workflows, and more ways to take advantage of FFmpeg without requiring users to work directly with the command line.

The goal is simple:

> **Make powerful video processing feel like a small, useful desktop tool.**
